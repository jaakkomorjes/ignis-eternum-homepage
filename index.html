IGNIS ETERNUM

Ignis Eternum draws its listeners into the depths of dark melodic death metal. The music is a blend of mysticism, grandiosity, and a harsh soundscape, all intertwined with classical vocals. The band draws its lyrical inspiration from Norse mythology. Ignis Eternum was formed in the Tampere region in 2018. The first EP was released in 2019, after which, years later, their debut album "Beginning of the End" saw the light of day in 2024. The band is currently working hard on new material, which will be heard at upcoming gigs in the near future.

From right to left, Ignis Eternum is

  • Teemu Mäkinen - Vocals
  • Laura Jokinen - Vocals
  • Paavo Lappi - Bass
  • Lauri Kaukovirta - Guitar
  • Jaakko Haataja - Guitar
  • Mika Toivo-Koivisto - Drums
